# Compare Peru booking-automation sectors

## Question

Using high-trust current sources, which appointment- or reservation-led local service sectors in Peru offer the best combination of demand, operational pain, monthly willingness to pay, sales accessibility, competitive opening, and MVP simplicity?

## Answer

The ranked desk-research result is: (1) multi-staff beauty salons, (2) private dental/specialist practices, (3) wellness clinics/spas, (4) veterinary clinics, (5) premium barbershops, and (6) reservation-led restaurants.

The leading concept is a constrained WhatsApp booking receptionist for multi-staff beauty salons. It offers the cleanest balance of observable sector formation, frequent bookings, simple appointment inventory, reachable owner-buyers, and relatively low compliance/integration burden. Private dental/specialist practices may have greater value per recovered slot, but they carry higher privacy, trust, integration, and incumbent pressure. Wellness/spas are attractive but lack strong Peru-specific growth evidence.

This is a research resolution, not a final build decision. Public sources do not establish local booking-channel mix, no-show rates, leakage, software penetration, or willingness to pay. Those require primary customer evidence.
