# Select Peru's first booking-automation MVP

## Destination

Reach an evidence-backed, commercially testable decision on the first local-service sector and narrow booking-automation MVP to pursue in Peru, including its buyer, price hypothesis, first-five-customer motion, risks, and explicit build/no-build gate.

## Notes

- Domain: Peru local-service booking and reservation automation, initially Lima Metropolitana.
- Every session should consult the `research`, `grilling`, and `domain-modeling` skills as applicable.
- Optimize for a practical monthly service that solves measurable operational loss; impressive technology is not a selection criterion.
- Separate official or first-party evidence, vendor claims, decision-model judgments, and unvalidated assumptions.
- Planning only: production implementation, outbound prospecting, and customer-data collection are outside this map unless the destination is explicitly redrawn.

## Decisions so far

- [Compare Peru booking-automation sectors](issues/01-compare-peru-booking-automation-sectors.md) — Current desk-research leader is a WhatsApp-first booking receptionist for multi-staff beauty salons; real workflow leakage and paid demand remain unverified.

## Not yet specified

- Exact WhatsApp Business Platform/provider architecture, message economics, and number-migration approach; specify only after paid demand and workflow fit are demonstrated.
- Whether deposits or payment links are necessary to reduce no-shows; revisit after baseline cancellation/no-show data exist.
- Whether inbound call overflow or requested callbacks justify voice automation; revisit after the messaging MVP establishes trust and unit economics.
- Production security, retention, and operational-support model; define after the chosen workflow and data boundary are stable.

## Out of scope

- Building production software before the commercial decision gate is met.
- Autonomous outbound sales calls, cold messaging, clinical advice or triage, medical records, restaurant table-allocation engines, POS/ERP replacement, payroll, inventory, loyalty, and marketplace discovery.
